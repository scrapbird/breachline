A workspace ties the files, annotations, and notes of an investigation together so you can save your progress, share it, and sync it across machines.

Workspaces, annotations, and sync are [licensed features](/docs/getting-started/licensing/).

- [Overview](/docs/workspaces/overview/) - what a workspace is and the dashboard.
- [Local Workspaces](/docs/workspaces/local-workspaces/) - the `.breachline` file on disk.
- [Sync Workspaces](/docs/workspaces/sync-workspaces/) - keeping a workspace in sync across machines.
- [Sync Account & Auth](/docs/workspaces/sync-account-auth/) - signing in and what is transmitted.
- [Annotations](/docs/workspaces/annotations/) - notes and colors on rows.
- [Exporting a Timeline](/docs/workspaces/exporting-a-timeline/) - merging annotated files into one file.
