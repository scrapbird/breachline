Welcome to the BreachLine documentation. These guides cover installing the application, loading data, querying during an investigation, and getting the most out of workspaces, annotations, plugins, and sync.

Use the navigation on the left (or the menu button on mobile) to browse the docs. If you're new here, start with [Installation](/docs/getting-started/installation/).

## Sections

- **Getting Started** - install BreachLine, activate a license, and load your first file.
- **Loading Data** - supported formats, JPATH expressions, and how timestamps and timezones work.
- **Searching & Analysis** - the query language, the timeline histogram, tabs, and copying results.
- **Workspaces** - local and sync workspaces, annotations, and exporting a timeline.
- **Configuration** - settings, keybindings, and the cache system.
- **Extending** - installing loader plugins and writing your own.
- **Reference** - security and data handling, plus troubleshooting.
